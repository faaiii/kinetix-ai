## 6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

- **Q: What happens when I hit my rolling 5-hour limit?**  
  *A:* Unlike black-box platforms that lock you out, Kinetix shows an exact cooldown timer and offers an instant BYOK toggle so you can plug in your own API key and continue without interruption.
- **Q: Can I run Kinetix completely offline for private corporate code?**  
  *A:* Yes. Kinetix features a native Local Model connector that routes prompts directly to Ollama or local LLM servers running on your machine with zero cloud telemetry.
- **Q: How does the Artifact canvas execute code safely?**  
  *A:* All code, HTML, and SVG artifacts render inside a strictly isolated iframe sandbox (`sandbox="allow-scripts"` without `allow-same-origin`), preventing any script from accessing your browser cookies or parent DOM.
- **Q: How easy is it to cancel my subscription?**  
  *A:* You can cancel anytime with one click in your account settings through our Stripe Customer Portal. No support tickets, no phone calls, and no dark patterns.
